# AMX Research Lab

An autonomous agent-driven team demonstrating device recognition, deployment orchestration, and proof-of-work validation using Hermes Local and Hermes Advanced Nous agents.

## Org Chart
* **Director** (Director) - manages the lab
  * **Hermes Sentinel** (Security and Local Deployments)
  * **Nous Specialist** (Advanced Tool Operations and MCP)

## Workflow
We use a **Hub-and-Spoke** workflow:
1. **Goal Assignment**: The Director assigns verification, deployment, or diagnostic tasks.
2. **Specialist Selection**: 
   - Local device and CLI validation is routed to the **Hermes Sentinel** (running locally on the device).
   - Multi-tool, browser-automation, or complex remote validations are routed to the **Nous Specialist** (running Hermes Advanced).
3. **Execution & Proof of Work**: Specialists perform tasks, generate a validation summary, and report outcomes to the Director.
